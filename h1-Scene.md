https://darknetdiaries.com/episode/178/
EP 178: UBIQUITI
- About Nickolas Sharp, who was a cloud engineer at Ubiquiti from 2018 until his arrest in 2021. 
- He was a leader of the cloud team, earning about 250,000 per year. 
- Fed up with the company not taking security problems seriously and thus not appreciating his work, he started to exploit the weaknesses of the company he was working for in the late 2020.
- With the help of Surfshark VPN, he used the company credentials and privileged credentials and shared accounts to pose as an outside attacker.
- This allowed him to download private GitHub repositories containing company information that was confidential. 
- After this he investigated his own attacks inside the company, while diverting the attention away from him. Also starting to extort the company for 2 million dollars worth in bitcoin at the time
- He was found with the real home IP address despite the usage of a VPN. After this he made himself a whistleblower, making more damage to the company
- In 2023 during February he pleaded guilty to the charges and was sentenced to six years in the prison.
- The episode highlights problems with shared credentials, excessive privileges, inadequate monitoring and insufficient logging.
